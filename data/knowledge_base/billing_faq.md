# Billing FAQ

## Refund Policy
Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days. Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, a prorated refund may be issued at management discretion.

## Duplicate Charges
If a customer reports a duplicate charge, verify the charge in the billing system before issuing a refund. Duplicate charges should be refunded immediately without requiring manager approval.

## Subscription Cancellation
Customers can cancel their subscription at any time from the Account Settings page. The subscription remains active until the end of the current billing cycle. No partial refunds are given for unused days in the current cycle.

## Payment Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and UPI payments. Customers can update their payment method in Account Settings > Billing.
